# Playable game: character, rifle, destructible wall, animated interface, sound, saves and network

A small game made only with the public API of the engine, to copy from. It is played in the scene
`assets/arena/main.scene`.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `project.mirai` | Name, version, identifier, icon, start scene (`arena/main.scene`), window and build options. |
| Actions | `move` (W A S D, left stick), `look` (mouse, right stick), `jump` (Space, South), `sprint` (Left Shift, left stick), `crouch` (Left Ctrl, right stick: a slide at a run), `fire` (left button, right trigger), `reload` (R, North), `use` (E, West), `save` (F5, D-pad up) and `load` (F9, Back), and the engine's `menu` (Esc, Start). |
| `settings.mirai` | `Spawn_Settings.prefab` names `assets/player/Player.scene`: the `spawn` plugin gives each player (the one of this machine and, on a server, the one of each connection) a body of it at the spawn point whose `players` is on, and destroys it when the player leaves. |
| `game/game.odin` | The game library. `Player` is the character component; `Status` is what the two interfaces show. `FIRE`, `RELOAD`, `USE`, `SAVE` and `LOAD` are its actions, whose keys `action_defaults` gives in code (`move` and `jump` belong to locomotion and `look` to the engine, which give theirs); `game_equip` puts the rifle a `Loadout` names in the hands of its player (`abilities.ability_wield`), and `game_shots` (`server`: only where the rules are decided) damages the wall where a shot hits it (the `Hit` of `Ability_Events`, with its point, and `destruct.damage`, which ignores what is not destructible); `game_shatter` (`server`) shatters the wall when a `USE` held at it completes (`scene.Interaction_Events`: the wall is a `scene.Interactable`, the player a `scene.Interactor` of `use`) and `game_prompt` tells the player on the HUD; `game_play` counts the hits the wall received (`Destruct_Events`); `game_saves` saves or loads the `quick` slot with `SAVE` and `LOAD`. `ATTRIBUTE_ENERGY`, `SPEED`, `JUMP` and `ACCELERATION` name the attributes of the player's `Ability_User`, `ROUNDS` the magazine of the rifle and `HEALTH` that of the target dummy, and `TAG_HEAD` its head zone; `game_ledge_jump` asks `locomotion.ledge_ahead` when jump is pressed at a run and uses the ability its `Ledge_Jump` names. |
| `game/shell.odin` | Main menu, pause (Esc, Start) and graphics settings, with mouse and keyboard. `Shell.page` is the page `assets/menu/shell.ui` shows, and its settings page binds the fields of `Graphics_Settings` and `Display_Settings` directly, so the engine holds and keeps what the player chose. Pause stops the clock; Quit to Menu restarts the scene (`scene.request_restart`). The `pause` of its Modal screen stops the clock on those pages; the engine holds and frees the mouse. |
| `tests/scenarios/shell.mirai` | Plays menu, Play, pause, a setting and Quit to Menu with real keys: `mirai play test <project> shell`. |
| `tests/scenarios/rifle.mirai` | Aims at the chest and at the head of the dummy and fires (25 and 100), and reloads: the magazine hides and the rounds come back. |
| `editor/editor.odin` | The engine-time package of the project, empty: its own tools and panels go there. It never enters the distributed game. |
| `tools/content/main.odin` | Writes what code generates with `content.Writer` (which also writes tables of game data and scenes from code): the badge, the hum and the shot with their WAVs, the rig of the target dummy (`assets/target`: three bones, a box on each, a sway that loops) and the reload clip of the rifle. `mirai content <project>` builds and runs it; a second run writes nothing. |
| `assets/arena/main.scene` | Floor, spawn point of the players (`Spawn_Point` with `players` on) with `Player.scene`, the wall (`Destructible`, 24 pieces, anchored to the floor; `Interactable` in `Look` mode, held 1 s), a speaker with a looping sound (`Audio_Source`), a sign over it (`World_Widget`), the HUD (`UI_Screen`) and the target dummy (its rig, a capsule `Collider`, an `Ability_User` with 100 health, `Hit_Zone_Set` with the zones of `dummy`, and `Rewind`, so a server judges a shot where the player who fired saw it). The sign and the HUD carry a `Status`. |
| `assets/player/Player.scene` | The character (`Character`, `Walk`, which walks it, jumps and makes it fall with the actions of the seat that drives it, `Slide`, `Move_Input`, `Player`, `Replicate`, `Interactor` of `use`, `Ability_User` with 115 energy that comes back at 52 a second 2 s after it was spent, `Ledge_Jump` and a `Loadout` with its rifle) with its camera at the eyes (a child with `Camera_Follow` in `Eyes` mode: the player looks and walks where it looks) and the rifle under it (a `Viewmodel` of 55 degrees, which the view of its player draws over the world, an `Ability_User` of its own with the kit `rifle` and 30 rounds, the `Animation` of `rifle.anim`, a body and a `Magazine`): what appears for each player and what a client creates for each replica. |
| `assets/weapon/rifle.records`, `rifle_hits.records`, `rifle_kit.records`, `zones.records` | The recipe of a hitscan rifle, rows of the `abilities` and `animation` plugins: `fire` (on `fire`, a round from `rounds`, 0.1 s of cooldown) opens the `shot` hitbox, a `Ray` from the aim of the seat 100 m long whose `on_begin` is the shot at its muzzle and whose `kick` raises the look 0.03 rad, then `recover` (a `Self` hitbox without an effect) brings 0.02 back; `shot` takes 25 `health`, and its `target_effects` row takes 100 from a hit in a zone tagged `head`; `reload` (on `reload`, below 30 rounds) plays the clip `reload` of the rifle, which hides the magazine, and at 0.85 s `refill` gives 30 rounds back. `zones.records` holds the head, chest and hips of the dummy. What the data does not say: rounds kept apart from the magazine (a reload always fills it), automatic fire while held other than a `Down` input, aim down sights, and the HUD of the rounds. |
| `assets/player/movement.records`, `movement_kit.records` | The recipe of a sprint with an energy bar and a jump off a ledge, rows of the `abilities` plugin (`records.inspect`): `sprint`, held on `sprint` in walking with 10 energy or more, spends 15 a second except while falling, ends after 1.5 s falling, and raises speed 1.4x and jump 1.2x (ramped in) and acceleration 3x; `ledge_jump` adds 8 m/s forward to a jump at 5 m/s or more. The kit `player` starts the sprint by input; the game uses the ledge jump. |
| `assets/hud/hud.ui`, `assets/arena/sign.ui`, `assets/menu/*.ui` | The source of the interfaces: `hud.ui` and `sign.ui` show `message` and `hits` of `Status` (the HUD has a badge that pulses, a named animation), `shell.ui` the pages of `game/shell.odin` and `theme.ui` the look they share, tokens and styles, and `button.ui`, `panel.ui`, `select_row.ui` and `check_row.ui` the widgets the screens use. `mirai rpc ui.inspect` reads them, `ui.write` changes them, the editor edits the same files. |
| `assets/hud/badge.tex` | The badge image. |
| `assets/arena/hum.wav` | The hum of the speaker, the source of `assets/arena/hum.sound`. |

To play: `mirai play <project>`, or Play in the editor (`mirai edit <project>`). With network:
`mirai play <project> --server` and, in another terminal,
`mirai play <project> --connect 127.0.0.1`. The server decides the hits and breaks the wall; each
client predicts its character. A network session does not save games. `mirai build game` builds
the game to distribute in `.mirai/game`.

To test how it plays: write each behavior as a scenario in `tests/scenarios/<name>.mirai` (the
scene, then steps that are calls of the play methods: input, ticks, checks) and run them all with
`mirai play test <project>`, which exits with 1 when one fails.

Escape or Start pauses the game; while the pause menu shows, the mouse is free. Escape again
resumes.
