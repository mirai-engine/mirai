## Workflow

1. Locate the contract in the Map. Before changing a pass, check in the frame graph what it reads
   and writes and which histories it uses.
2. When adding or changing a stage, keep its `stage_contract` equal to what it reads and writes and
   mark optional only what the stage replaces when missing. A stage that a pipeline can omit leaves
   in the `Pass` a value that the following ones can use (`view_begin`).
3. Shaders: edit the Slang source; `mirai check`, `test` and `build` regenerate the outputs, which
   git does not store.
4. What only some games use enters through the extension API (`render/extend.odin`, exposed by
   `engine/render`), added together with its first consumer.
5. A change in one backend is replicated in the other with the same signature. Metal is checked
   with `mirai check rhi -target:darwin_arm64`; its execution, only on a Mac.
6. Update the affected reference in `references/`.

## Invariants

- The shader language is Slang: a single source, SPIR-V for Vulkan and MSL for Metal generated from
  that SPIR-V with SPIRV-Cross.
- A constant the shaders share with the CPU has one declaration, in Odin (`render/abi`, `asset`,
  `rhi`; a plugin's, in its own code), and the shaders get it generated
  ([shared constants](references/shaders.md#compilation)): no `.slang` source writes its value.
- The graphics API is the own `rhi` over Vulkan and Metal; wgpu is not used.
- Each shader stage compiles in a compile request of its own, from the command line of slangc,
  in compilers that load Slang once and outlive it (`mirai shaders compiler`), even if other
  stages have the same defines.
- A Surface program is compiled only for what it is drawn as (`abi.Program_Use`): what the assets
  of the project draw it as, what its source declares and, in the editor, the previews it shows.
  A draw it was not compiled for is a problem of the frame that says how to declare it.
- What a pass chooses of a stage is a constant of its pipelines, not another compilation; a
  variant is only for what the stage declares to the device
  ([constants and variants](references/shaders.md#compilation)).
- The engine has no Surface program: those of the tests are in `render/testdata/`.
- A view's direct light costs its samples, not its lights: no pixel traces or reads one per light.
- The maps of the shadows draw only what changed, in the frame it changed: no map shows a caster
  or a light where it was in a frame before ([cache](references/lights.md#direct-light-of-a-view)),
  but a page that waits for room to follow a batch that sways, which shows the sway it last drew,
  and one that waits to take a surface at another detail or a batch past its cull distance, which
  shows what it last drew, never a lit page or a hole. A surface shown at another detail draws
  again only the pages whose texel is finer than its error. What sways obeys the bound of the
  frame like the rest, and its plan looks at no batch while none starts or stops swaying.
- Shadows of what sways move only where they read: within a fixed reach of the eye
  (`SHADOW_SWAY_REACH`), never farther because the frame has time to spare; small plants cast at
  rest. What sways is drawn a fixed number of levels coarser (`SHADOW_SWAY_COARSER`) and the atlas
  takes the nearer of it and the cache: what stays is exact. What the vertex hook sways has no
  swayed copy: rays meet it at rest, and a point shown swayed is tested against what rests from
  where it rests, never by skipping what lies near.
- Shadows are never colored or darkened by the glass they pass, in the maps or in the rays: colored
  light through glass comes from the light, one that projects a texture.
- A decal changes the surface under it before it is lit and is never lit itself: no decal is a
  forward blend. Its order is decided once, on change, the same in every view; a frame looks at
  no entry. Without a decal in the scene and without the `Decal` stage a view has no decal target,
  no decal pass and the resolve of before ([decals](references/materials.md#decals)).
- Forward blends trace no shadow rays: the maps of the view shadow them, they mark the pages they
  read, and a light that no map holds at a point reaches it whole.
- What is decided per instance (its class, the row of its material with its own values) is built
  on the CPU when the instance is given: no stage builds it again per pixel or per hit.
- A graphics option keeps a choice, and a preset a step above another, only where the image
  differs to the eye: what costs more and looks the same is not offered.
- The display transform is a project setting, Neutral by default and ACES 2.0 by choice, baked
  into one LUT. Every view is exposed above its meter by the bias of its transform, one that gives
  the meter's gray the same light under each; the ACES 2.0 calibration is not touched.
- `render` does not see native handles: backend differences stay in `rhi`, with a single
  implementation of each pass for Vulkan and Metal. A plugin imports neither `render` nor `rhi`
  outside its tests, in its runtime or in its editor part: `mirai check` fails on it.
- A GPU loss neither aborts nor hangs: `rhi` stores it typed (`device_failure`) from the first
  point that reports it, and everything that submits or waits returns at once; no Vulkan wait
  exceeds `GPU_WAIT_LIMIT_NS`.
- Device and renderer receive allocator and capacities in their `*_Desc`. The generated shader
  tables are `@(rodata)`.
- On Vulkan, calls go through the device tables (`d.dvk`, `d.ivk`, `c.device.dvk`), never through
  the `vendor:vulkan` pointers.
- A stable frame neither allocates memory nor creates GPU resources. What exceeds a capacity is
  discarded and counted (`rhi.device_stats`, `render.Stats`).
- The renderer draws relative to an origin that jumps: on the CPU a world place passes to it only
  with `origin_point` and the other functions of `render/origin.odin` (which `engine/render`
  exposes); in a shader, what the origin adds to a world place comes only from `origin.slang`, and
  a place is projected only with `view_clip`.
- Split screen shares scene, pipelines and probes; each view keeps targets, matrices and histories.
  The screen UI is composed once; panels and lines, in each view.
- Visual copies use `copy_owner`/`copy_variant` and `uniform_color` without changing the object ID
  or adding geometry or materials.
- Batch instances enter through the same cull, G-buffer and tracing as the scene ones; a shader
  reads an instance by index only with `scene_instance`.
- A geometry regeneration registers an immutable version and withdraws the previous one, with free
  ranges and dirty ranges of the common pools, without compacting other geometry.
- `render_sync` gives the renderer the world only by what changed: the rows written, added or gone
  of the tables it reads, through `scene_update`, `scene_move`, `scene_tint`, `scene_light_update`
  and `scene_light_remove`.
  Another world, tables replaced whole and assets loaded again are the same changes, of every row:
  there is no second path that builds the scene. A frame where nothing changed looks at no row.
- Geometry publication is a common immediate transaction with reused staging, without
  `buffer_upload` or waits per pool; its cost counts in `cpu_prepare_ms`.
- What is drawn over the finished image enters through `Frame_Desc` and does not change what a
  frame that does not use it records.
- The skinning palette already includes the inverse bind: the renderer does not apply it again.
- Colored light through a window comes from a light that projects a texture
  ([lights](references/lights.md#local-lights)); a light's color at a point comes only from
  `light_contribution`.
- Every light enters in render units in a single place ([units](references/display.md#units)): no
  shader adds constant light or reads an intensity without converting.
- The light that arrives around a point is composed only with `indirect_compose`. The sun's at a
  place (a surface, the fog and its distant slices) comes only from `sun_light` (`scene.slang`),
  which makes it cross the air of an atmosphere.
- The indirect samples nothing per frame: the probes always trace the same directions, contact
  occlusion a fixed pattern and the direct light samples of fixed places. What the scene
  does not change gives the same every frame; there is no on-screen history of the indirect or
  bounce factor, and the scene changes are marked by `Scene_State.changes`
  ([GI](references/gi.md#temporal-and-denoising)).
- Another world, or the same one replaced by another scene, is announced with `scene_world` before
  its scene is given; without it, what the scene is given is only an edit.
- No engine stage evaluates a Surface program except its own (`//! per surface program`): raster
  uses the program's pipeline (`program_graphics`) and rays ask for it through the material points
  (`points.slang`). The renderer starts with the pipelines of every program of its shaders: no
  frame of a game draws a program as its row; one edited since draws as its row until its
  pipeline is ready. There is no setting of when shaders compile. Variants that only spare work
  are speculative jobs, made after startup, cheapest first, by one thread alone; the others are
  reserved for what frames request. With a window, startup compilation runs off the window
  thread while the UI pipeline, made first, draws progress, Cancel and the window buttons.
  Cancel or closing during a driver compile writes the cache and exits.
- A masked material is cut by its mask map alone: no visibility raster, shadow map or traversal
  runs a program for coverage ([alpha](references/materials.md#alpha)).
- The material of opaque scene geometry runs once per visible pixel: its clusters are rasterized
  into the visibility target and resolved per program; no forward path draws them into the
  G-buffer. Back faces are drawn only of two-sided materials.
- The passes read the G-buffer only with `include/gbuffer.slang`. A material's layers are prepared
  only with `shading_layers`, and direct and indirect light compose them the same.
- A scene's image reaches the display only through the display LUT (`display_encode`); previews,
  thumbnails and the UI studio, only through Khronos PBR Neutral (`preview_encode`). No shader
  applies another tone curve.

## Verification

- **A change of light, GI, materials, exposure or render cost is accepted only with
  `mirai render check demo` without regressions against `main` and without load warnings**
  ([check](references/verification.md#render-check)), besides the reference metrics of `gi_test`;
  `--quality` is enough when the change cannot move the cost.
  Fixtures are not enough to assert quality or cost.
- GI is also judged in motion: the blotches of the flying views of the check (`main_wall`
  sideways), not only the still stability and the trail.
- Test only the affected packages (`rhi`, `rhi/tests`, `render`, `engine/render`, `cook/shaders`)
  with `MIRAI_VALIDATION=1` (Vulkan) or `MTL_DEBUG_LAYER=1` (Metal). No GPU test may report
  validation errors; a new one takes the shared device
  ([GPU environment](references/verification.md#gpu-environment)).
- A change of `render_sync` runs `engine/render/sync_test.odin`, without a device: what the
  renderer is given in a frame (the instances it touched, the light it marks) and what it has
  against a world given whole.
- A change that must preserve the image is checked with `MIRAI_GOLDEN=write:<dir>` before and
  `MIRAI_GOLDEN=compare:<dir>` after, on the same machine.
- After regenerating shaders, the diff contains only the outputs of the touched sources, the
  manifest, the catalog and the table fingerprint.
- Device loss: `rhi/tests/rhi_test.odin` and `host/gpu_failure_test.odin`; in a session,
  `engine.gpu_lose`. Metal, on a Mac: the same ones of `rhi/tests` with `MTL_DEBUG_LAYER=1`.
  Device choice and a lost window surface: `rhi/vulkan_pick_test.odin` and
  `host/surface_lost_test.odin`.
- Device memory: `rhi/arena_test.odin` and `rhi/vulkan_memory_test.odin` (without a device) and
  `rhi/tests/pool_test.odin`.
- Extensions: scenes of the render plugins. Benchmarks: only when touching a hot path.
- Frame generation: `host/frame_generation_test.odin` (with a window), `rhi/present_test.odin` and
  the tests of `plugins/fsr/runtime`; in an editor with a window (`mirai edit`: a headless session
  does not present), `presentation` of `profile.capture` with and without `frame_generation` and
  `capture` with `ui` and `generated` against the same view without generation.
- Surface programs: the focal tests of
  [programs](references/materials.md#program-limits) (`render`), and `generate_test.odin` and
  `programs_test.odin` of `cook/shaders` for the per-program stages and the coverage; in a session,
  `material.program.edit` with `material.preview.capture` and `capture`.
- Texture residency: the tests of `render/texture_residency_test.odin` (CPU) and, with GPU,
  `host/assets_texture_test.odin`; in the editor, `textures` of
  `engine.status` (memory, pending) when bringing the camera close.
- Environment, cases with their own conditions and measurement:
  [verification](references/verification.md).
